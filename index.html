Kyuhwan Shim

Research Scientist @ LG AI Research

Biography

I am a Research Scientist in the Physical Intelligence Lab at LG AI Research , where I study embodied intelligence and its applications to real-world agents. I am also pursuing a master's degree at the Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang.

My research focuses on developing machine learning methods for robust Physical Intelligence through multimodal learning. In particular, I study how embodied agents can explore unfamiliar environments, adapt to changing conditions, and continuously refine their actions from interaction. My interests include vision-language-action models, language-conditioned robot policies, data-efficient imitation learning, and interpretable action generation.

My work includes RAIN, a region-aware approach to robotic task generalization accepted to the X-Reason Workshop at ECCV 2026; CLIP-RT, which learns language-conditioned robotic policies from natural language supervision and was presented orally at Robotics: Science and Systems 2025; ESPADA, a semantics-aware demonstration downsampling method published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters; and MoAQ, an approach to interpreting action policies through mixtures of action queries. Across these projects, my goal is to develop agents that learn efficiently, generalize across diverse tasks, and act in interpretable, reliable, and human-aligned ways.

Previously, I was a Visiting Student Researcher in the Autonomous Intelligent Systems Group at the University of Bonn and a Vision Engineer at TIDYBOY-DSPL. I also worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Nota and co-founded OCONNECT with my CNSH colleagues. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Sogang University .
